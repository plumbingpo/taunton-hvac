No Surprises

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Taunton HVAC provides honest, upfront pricing across Taunton, covering everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a clear recommendation and a written price before any work starts.

The problem with HVAC failures is that most of them develop where you can't see them. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil slowly drains refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down gradually, and a weak capacitor holds on until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. When bills climb, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, have the system checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most air conditioners and furnaces last 12 to 18 years, and a planned replacement is nearly always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap during a heat wave or cold snap. Our technicians know the climate demands common to Taunton and how they affect equipment life, so we can tell you honestly whether your system has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Preventive Care

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Each maintenance visit from Taunton HVAC is more than a filter change. Our technician cleans the coils, verifies the refrigerant charge, tests capacitors and contactors, inspects the burners and heat exchanger, clears the condensate drain, replaces the filter, and checks static pressure in the duct system. Small problems are caught while they're still cheap to fix, and the equipment stays running clean and efficient.

Every maintenance plan we write is a little different. A furnace in its later years needs a closer look than a new heat pump, and a dusty, high-pollen area means condenser coils foul faster. We build spring cooling and fall heating visits around your equipment and climate, so every tune-up helps keep the system efficient and extends the life of the equipment you've already paid for.

The savings show up quickly. Clean coils move heat with less effort, a correct refrigerant charge takes strain off the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Compared with the cost of replacing a system even one year early, a maintenance plan is money well spent.

Right-Sized Systems

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

Getting the size right is one of the most overlooked parts of any install. Before recommending new equipment, Taunton HVAC measures the details that drive your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. The result is a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, keeps humidity in check, and keeps every room at the temperature you set.

Houses change over time. A finished basement, an addition, or two new bedrooms can overwhelm the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use far less energy. If you're torn between a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll lay out the honest pros and cons in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Repairs We Handle

The HVAC Repairs Taunton Homes Need Most

Warm air from the vents, a furnace that won't ignite, ice on the indoor coil, a slow refrigerant leak: every one of these problems has its own cause and calls for a different repair. Taunton HVAC diagnoses the exact fault before swapping any parts, whether it's a condenser that hums without starting, a short-cycling furnace, a thermostat that loses its settings, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split flashing error codes.

Some problems are mostly a nuisance, while others pose real risks. A weak capacitor simply keeps the compressor from starting, but a cracked heat exchanger can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling. We match the fix to the failure: capacitors, contactors, and fan motors on cooling systems, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards on furnaces, sealed and repaired ductwork where conditioned air is bleeding into the attic, and a proper refrigerant recharge after a repaired leak. Whatever you're facing, our team has the parts and experience to handle it.
